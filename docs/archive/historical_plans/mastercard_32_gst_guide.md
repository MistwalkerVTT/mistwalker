## ⚡ Fast Batching in QBO (Takes Under 60 Seconds):

1. **Batch Select Canadian Vendors:**  
   Click the checkboxes for all your **Google, Regus, Rogers, Fuel, and Parking** items.  
   Click **Update** at the top bar -> set **Tax:** `GST` -> click **Apply and accept**.
2. **Batch Select US Software:**  
   Click the checkboxes for **Apollo, Dropbox, Gamma, and DJI**.  
   Click **Update** -> set **Tax:** `Zero-rated` (or `Out of Scope`) -> click **Apply and accept**.
3. **HUB Insurance:**  
   Select the two HUB International rows -> set **Tax:** `Exempt` -> click **Apply and accept**.
