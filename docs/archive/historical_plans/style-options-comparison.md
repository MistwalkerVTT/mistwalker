# Active Drone Solutions – Design Options Overview

You noted that the previous rebuild felt like it was trying too hard to look "high tech" with dark gradients and glowing accents. We have stripped away all futuristic/cyber gimmicks (no neon text gradients, no emojis, no glowing cards) and rebuilt the aesthetic around **authentic, credible industrial contracting in Alberta**.

A live **Style Options Switcher** has been installed at the bottom of every page on `http://localhost:8080/` so you can instantly switch between the three options in your browser with one click.

---

## The 3 Design Options

### Option 1: Faithful 1:1 Original Polish *(Currently Active by Default)*
* **Visual Direction:** An exact, clean recreation of your live GoDaddy website layout and brand personality, rebuilt with clean, lightning-fast static code.
* **Typography:** Bold uppercase headings in `Archivo Black` paired with `Montserrat` for body text.
* **Color Palette:** Pure white (`#ffffff`) alternating with soft light grey (`#f6f6f6`) section backgrounds, with your exact signature brand green (`#439c48`).
* **Buttons & Elements:** Classic pill-shaped rounded buttons (`border-radius: 48px`), clean 1px borders, original clean flat cards.
* **Tone:** Honest, grounded, established Canadian drone inspection contractor.

---

### Option 2: Modern Canadian Industrial Contractor
* **Visual Direction:** Inspired by leading Canadian engineering, surveying, and pipeline contractors (such as Stantec, GeoVerra, and Ledcor).
* **Typography:** Crisp architectural typography using `Inter` and `Plus Jakarta Sans`.
* **Color Palette:** Clean white backgrounds with architectural slate (`#f8fafc`), deep charcoal headings (`#1e293b`), and spruce forest green (`#2e7d32`).
* **Buttons & Elements:** Structured rectangular cards with subtle 4px rounded corners, 1px divider lines, and clean technical metric badges.
* **Tone:** Serious, high-credibility corporate engineering firm focused on asset integrity, safety records, and regulatory compliance.

---

### Option 3: Clean Field Photography & Survey Showcase
* **Visual Direction:** Understated, open, and photo-forward (similar to Leica Geosystems or aerial survey field journals).
* **Typography:** `Outfit` geometric titles with `Inter` body text.
* **Color Palette:** Warm white (`#ffffff`) and soft sand grey (`#faf9f6`), with muted natural sage/forest green (`#3b7a57`).
* **Buttons & Elements:** Generous whitespace, borderless image cards, and clean typography that lets your actual field photography and LiDAR point clouds do the talking.
* **Tone:** Premium, calm, confident aerial intelligence provider.
