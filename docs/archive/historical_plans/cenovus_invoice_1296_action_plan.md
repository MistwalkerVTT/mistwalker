## 🔍 Why Did Cenovus Short-Pay the Taxes?

Large enterprise energy operators (like Cenovus) commonly do this for one of three reasons:
1. **AP Automation / System Glitch:** Their ERP (SAP / OpenInvoice) matched the Purchase Order line items (\$137,054.08) but failed to flag or release the separate tax code line.
2. **Missing GST Registration Number:** If your GST/HST registration number was omitted from the top header or PO field on Invoice 1296, Cenovus AP will withhold sales tax payment by default until verified.
3. **Self-Assessment / Direct Remittance Misclassification:** Oil & gas majors occasionally code invoices under an exemption rule or provincial tax exemption erroneously.

2. **Leave Open Balance:** Invoice 1296 will remain in your **Accounts Receivable (A/R)** with an open unpaid balance of exactly **\$6,852.71**.
3. **CRA Protection:** When Cenovus remits the remaining \$6,852.71, you simply apply the second payment to close the invoice to \$0.00.
